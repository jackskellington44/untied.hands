Based between cities, I work in fine-line black and grey with an eye for the delicate and the strange. Heavily influenced by botanical illustration, antique medical diagrams, and the quiet weight of things that outlast us. Every piece is drawn freehand to your body — no stencil, no sticker. My work tends toward the intimate and the minimal: a single stem, a moth, a hand reaching for something just out of frame. I've been tattooing for eight years and I still find it astonishing every time. Inquiries open when they open.

deposits
A non-refundable deposit of $100 is required to hold your appointment. This goes toward the final price. Deposits are forfeited if you cancel within 48 hours of your appointment or no-show.

reschedules
One reschedule is allowed with at least 72 hours notice. Additional reschedules may require a new deposit.

design
I design everything from scratch based on our consultation. I do not copy other artists' work. Reference images are welcome and helpful. Final design is revealed at the appointment — no previews sent in advance.

placement
I reserve the right to decline placements I don't feel are suited to the design. Please come to your appointment with the area clean and accessible.

touch-ups
One complimentary touch-up is included within three months of your appointment. Touch-ups after that are priced at an hourly rate.

age
18+ only. No exceptions.
